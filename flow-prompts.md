# Google Flow image prompts

Generate these in Flow on your PC, then save them with the exact filenames below into `assets/images/`.
The site already points at these files; until they exist, gradient placeholders show.
Style for all: modern, cinematic, vibrant purple/magenta/teal lighting, shallow depth of field, no text or logos.
