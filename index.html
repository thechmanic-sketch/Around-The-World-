FAQ

How much space do you need?

About 3m × 3m with a clear height of 2.5m, plus a nearby power socket.

Do you deliver to my venue?

Yes. Delivery and setup are included within your local area; a small fee may apply further out.

Can you add our branding?

Yes. Custom overlays, intros and outros are available. Just tell us what you need in your enquiry.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry below. Once your date is confirmed, a deposit secures the booking. The opening special rate applies to bookings made while it runs.
