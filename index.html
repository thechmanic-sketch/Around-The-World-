Every speaker gets a fair turn

Choose a slot from 1 to 7 minutes. The countdown appears for everyone in the call, turns amber at the last minute, red at the last 15 seconds, then shows how far over time the speaker is.

Questions

Is TalentTrade free?

Yes. Starting and joining meetings is free. Sponsor ads help keep it that way.

Do my guests need an account or an app?

No. They open your invite link, type their name and join from their web browser.

Which devices does it work on?

Computers, tablets and phones with an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Allow camera and microphone access when the browser asks.

How does the presentation timer work?

In a call, tap Timer and choose 1 to 7 minutes. Everyone sees the same countdown, and anyone can pause, restart or stop it.
