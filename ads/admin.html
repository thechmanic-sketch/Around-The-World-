Sponsor ads manager

Set up the sponsor videos that play in Around The World Meet: who they're for, where they appear, when they run and how often.

How publishing works
  1. Put each sponsor video (MP4, ideally under 5 MB) in the repo's ads/videos/ folder, or use a full https:// link to a video hosted elsewhere.
  2. Edit the ads below. Your changes are kept in this browser as a draft until you download them.
  3. Click Download ads.json and replace ads/ads.json in the GitHub repo with it. The site updates on the next deploy.
